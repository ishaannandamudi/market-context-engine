# Canonical ingestion

Provider adapters translate vendor/file records into `BarObservation`, `QuoteObservation`, or `TradeObservation`. `normalize_observation` validates raw mapping fields with the canonical model and retains an original symbol as `provider_symbol`; a malformed record raises `ProviderMalformedDataError`. It does not fill missing values, alter timestamps, or repair OHLC. Canonical symbols are trimmed and uppercased but punctuation and internal spaces are preserved.

MCE-1 bars without interval bounds are still valid model instances for backward compatibility. MCE-2 ingestion and replay reject them: a complete bar must carry `bar_start_ns` and `bar_end_ns`, its event time must lie within the interval, and `effective_timestamp_ns >= bar_end_ns`. Providers must add publication/delivery delay to effective time where relevant. Ingestion classifies a bar's session by its start, so a final regular bar stamped at exact close is still regular. A bar crossing session categories is retained with `TIMESTAMP_MISMATCH` (or rejected when extended hours are disallowed). Trades and quotes may have optional provider sequences; equal timestamps do not establish duplicate identity.

`IngestionService.ingest` sorts a batch by effective time, event time, source, symbol, record type, then canonical JSON bytes. Exact repeated bars and quotes are counted as duplicates; trades are retained even when byte-identical. Missing-bar-bounds and disallowed extended-hours records are counted as rejected with flags. Off-session data on known closed dates is retained with `OUTSIDE_SESSION`, and out-of-calendar-range data is retained with `SESSION_UNKNOWN`. The service never silently drops a record. `IngestionResult` reports accepted/rejected/duplicate counts, accepted symbols, effective timestamp range, and flags. Duplicate-only batches do not imply missing data.

`ingest_provider` fetches only capabilities a provider declares, optionally restricted to requested capabilities. Missing requested capabilities and partial symbol coverage are flagged without blocking available data. An unavailable provider raises `ProviderUnavailableError`; authentication, entitlement, timeout, rate limit, and malformed data have distinct provider error categories. Other adapter exceptions are wrapped as `ProviderError` before reaching downstream code. Empty results represent no data, not a zero-valued observation.

`InMemoryHistoricalProvider` is a deterministic local reference implementation with fixed bars, quotes, and trades, controlled health/capabilities, and optional failure injection. It indexes records by symbol. Parquet I/O was deferred because adding pyarrow solely for this phase would increase dependency weight; it can be built behind the same protocols. Live late-arrival correction, provider-native sequence rules, and broad forward filling are also deferred. There is **no implicit forward fill**; any later policy must state allowed record type, maximum age, reason, and quality effect.
