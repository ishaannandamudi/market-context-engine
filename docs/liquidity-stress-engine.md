# Liquidity and market stress (MCE-9)

`LiquidityStressFeatureEngine.compute(store, as_of_ns, ...)` produces immutable `LiquidityStressFeatureSet` results. It reads local causal bars and quotes and a point-in-time `UniverseProvider`. The optional volatility, breadth, sector, correlation, and dispersion results must have the **same** `as_of_ns`; the engine never recomputes those engines. A supplied tuple of `StressHistoryPoint` records provides prior stress observations. There is no network or trained model.

## Clock, universe, and supported data

Every bar must belong wholly to the actual XNYS regular session, end by the explicit clock, and be effectively available then. The bar close is the representative traded price. Only the configured provider source is used. Cumulative volume requires a contiguous cadence-bounded bar sequence from the regular open through the last completed cadence boundary; a missing interval makes cumulative measurements unavailable rather than understating them. The latest completed bar must also be within the configured bar freshness limit; a stale or missing member has `None` measurements and remains in the expected member count. An explicit completed zero-volume bar is counted as observed volume zero, while Amihud remains unavailable. A halt with no fresh bar becomes stale or incomplete. At an incomplete opening minute, there is no bar-based liquidity result. The previous close must be the bar ending exactly at the previous **trading** session close; a missing close makes the gap unavailable. Weekends, holidays, and early closes follow the exchange calendar.

`MembershipTimeline` selects records with `effective_from_ns <= T < effective_to_ns` (or no end) and `available_at_ns <= T`. It also supplies the weights at `T`. The historical volume/Amihud/gap baseline obtains a fresh membership snapshot at each historical cutoff. Thus later additions, removals, and weight changes cannot revise an earlier result. A partial universe does not turn missing symbols into zero volume. `member_coverage = valid current bar members / expected members`; quote and optional weighted Amihud coverage are separate. The universe and quote coverage thresholds gate their respective aggregates.

## Liquidity mathematics

The current interval starts at the regular open and ends at `T` (capped at regular close). Only completed bars count. For member `i`:

| Output | Exact calculation | Units and missing rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `session_volume` | Sum of available completed regular-bar volumes | Shares. Missing without a fresh completed bar; observed zero remains zero. |
| `session_dollar_volume` | Sum of `bar.close × bar.volume` | Currency units of input prices. Missing without a fresh bar. |
| `session_return` | `latest completed close / first completed opening-bar open − 1` | Fraction. Requires a bar starting exactly at session open and positive open. |
| `illiquidity_proxy` / `price_impact_proxy` | `abs(session_return) / session_dollar_volume` | Fraction per currency unit, Amihud-style. Requires positive dollar volume and session return. It is a descriptive price-response proxy, not realized impact or transaction cost. |
| `relative_volume` | Current member cumulative volume divided by median of that member’s prior cumulative volumes at the **same elapsed time since open**; broad output is the median of eligible member ratios | Nonnegative ratio. At least `minimum_history_samples` member baselines, positive baseline, and minimum current member/RVOL coverage. |
| `relative_volume_5d` / `relative_volume_20d` | Same member ratio using exactly the most recent 5 or 20 comparable prior sessions, followed by the cross-sectional median | Nonnegative ratios. Each member needs the full respective prior window, a positive median baseline, and the configured current member coverage. These terms are diagnostics and do not enter the headline score. |
| `quoted_spread` | `ask − bid` for the latest fresh regular-session quote; broad output is the median | Input price units. Requires bid > 0, ask >= bid, positive finite midpoint, matching source, causal availability, and minimum quote coverage. After-hours quotes are excluded. |
| `relative_quoted_spread` | `(ask − bid) / ((ask + bid)/2)`; broad output is the median | Fraction. No quote or invalid/stale quote yields `None`; no OHLC or Roll spread is fabricated. |
| `turnover` | Unavailable | Always `None`; share float/outstanding data does not exist. |

Historical comparisons use up to `historical_sessions` prior XNYS sessions (default 20), the same elapsed nanoseconds from each historical regular open, and only data available at that historical cutoff. If a prior early close occurs before the comparison cutoff, that day is skipped. This is a simple same-clock baseline, **not** a fully fitted intraday seasonality curve; MCE-16 adds a separate completed-bucket normalized RVOL. A requested historical window shorter than the current elapsed interval may yield too few samples. The current observation is never included in its baseline.

For current market volume, the sum of member volumes is ranked against prior complete point-in-time universe sums; prior days with partial membership data are excluded from this broad baseline. Current market Amihud is the median of available member proxies; its baseline is the median on each prior day. `volume_percentile`, `illiquidity_percentile`, and member illiquidity percentiles are prior-only midranks: `(number below current + 0.5 × number equal) / N`. `volume_robust_z` and `illiquidity_robust_z` are `(current − prior median)/(1.4826 × prior MAD)`; equal constant histories give zero, while a different current value with zero MAD gives `None`. These require `minimum_history_samples` (default 5). `volume_breadth` is the fraction of comparable members with RVOL > 1. Cross-sectional dispersion is the population standard deviation of valid member RVOL, Amihud, or relative quoted spread. The raw market volume and dollar volume remain available even when history is insufficient.

`weighted_illiquidity_proxy` is the average of valid member Amihud values using the **current point-in-time weights**. It exists only if every active member has a nonnegative weight and the weight of valid Amihud members divided by total positive weight reaches `minimum_weight_coverage`. It is separate from the robust equal-member median used in the score. `dollar_volume_hhi = Σ(dollar_volume_i / Σ dollar_volume)^2` and `effective_active_names = 1/HHI` for positive total dollar volume. The concentration denominator uses observed members only and should be read with `member_coverage`.

For volume–price context, `large_move_weak_volume_fraction` counts members with `abs(session_return) >= large_move_threshold` and `RVOL < weak_volume_rvol_threshold`, divided by members with both return and RVOL. `large_move_high_volume_fraction` uses `RVOL > high_volume_rvol_threshold` with the same denominator. Defaults are 1% absolute move, 0.75 weak RVOL, and 2 high RVOL. Both require `minimum_members` comparable members and configured member coverage; otherwise they are `None`. They are descriptive fractions in `[0,1]`, not signals or score terms.

`liquidity_change = (prior median Amihud − current median Amihud)/(prior median + current median)` when both exist; zero denominator yields zero. Positive means improved liquidity. The change is an observed comparison, not a forecast. `liquidity_score` is higher for **better** liquidity: available terms are `1 − illiquidity_percentile` (default weight .45), `RVOL/(1+RVOL)` (.35), and `1/(1+relative_quoted_spread/spread_scale)` (.20; default scale .001). Terms are included only when valid and their configured weight is positive. If at least `minimum_score_components` (default 2) exist, active weights are divided by their sum and the score is their weighted sum in `[0,1]`. `active_weights` and contributors expose this renormalization; contributor values sum to the score. Otherwise the score is `None`. `liquidity_stress = 1 − liquidity_score` is a separate stress component. Coverage affects quality and component availability rather than masquerading as favorable liquidity.

## Gaps and continuous market stress

The gap for member `i` is `ln(first opening-bar open / previous trading-session completed close)`; it requires positive prices and a completed opening bar. Up/down fractions are counts of positive/negative gaps divided by valid gap count. `gap_magnitude` is median absolute gap, `gap_dispersion` is population standard deviation of signed gaps, and `gap_percentile` midranks current median absolute gap against prior same-clock point-in-time market medians. `gap_stress` exists only when gap member coverage meets the configured threshold: `down_fraction × min(1, median magnitude of negative gaps / gap_scale) × historical_factor`, with `historical_factor = .5 + .5 × gap_percentile` if available, otherwise 1. Default `gap_scale` is .02 log-return units. Positive gaps do not increase this asymmetric component. A market without valid gaps has `None`, never a fabricated calm zero.

The stress composite consumes one representative score from each evidence family, avoiding multiple full-weight volatility or correlation measurements:

| Component | Exact mapping | Default weight |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| `volatility` | Existing `volatility_score` | .22 |
| `correlation` | Existing `systemic_co_movement_score` | .17 |
| `breadth` | `(1 − breadth_score)/2`, so negative breadth raises stress | .20 |
| `liquidity` | `1 − liquidity_score` | .18 |
| `gap` | Asymmetric `gap_stress` above | .13 |
| `dispersion` | Existing `dispersion_score × breadth_stress`; dispersion with strong positive breadth contributes little | .06 |
| `sector` | Fraction of available sector medium-horizon absolute returns below zero, requiring the configured minimum valid sectors | .04 |

All component values are in `[0,1]`. The configured weights sum to one; only valid positive-weight components are active. With at least `minimum_context_components` (default 2), `stress_score = Σ(active component × active weight)/Σ(active weights)`. Each `stress.<component>` contributor records its exact contribution, and `active_weights` records the normalized weights. If there are too few components, score and contributors are unavailable. A high score indicates **observed evidence of broad strain**, not crash probability, loss probability, a forecast, or a trade recommendation. No categorical regime is assigned.

`stressed_member_fraction` is the fraction of comparable members with either Amihud percentile at or above `stress_breadth_threshold` (default .8) or a negative gap below `−gap_scale`. Members with neither comparable Amihud nor gap are excluded from the fraction; the metric needs at least `minimum_members` comparable members. `systemic_stress = stress_score × stressed_member_fraction`; `localized_stress = stress_score × (1 − stressed_member_fraction)`. These are descriptive partitions of the composite by breadth of member evidence, not independent probabilities or regime labels.

## Prior stress dynamics

The caller supplies immutable `StressHistoryPoint(as_of_ns, available_at_ns, stress_score)` records. Only points with observation time strictly before `T` and availability at or before `T` are considered; duplicate accepted timestamps are rejected. The most recent `historical_baseline_observations` (default 20) are used. A minimum of `minimum_history_samples` (default 5) yields the current stress midrank percentile and prior median. `stress_change = current − most recent prior`, and `stress_acceleration = current − 2 × most recent prior + second most recent prior`; these only need one/two prior points. `stress_persistence` is the fraction of the current plus the previous `persistence_observations − 1` scores above the prior baseline median, requiring the full lookback and a valid baseline. `stress_shock_score = max(0, 2×percentile−1) × max(0, current−prior median)/(abs(current)+abs(prior median))`, with the last ratio zero when its denominator is zero. Future stress history cannot alter a past result.

## Quality, snapshot, and boundaries

The typed result exposes expected/valid members, member/quote/weight coverage, stale bar/quote counts, source quality, evidence quality, and inherited quality flags. Liquidity evidence quality is `member_coverage × membership_quality_factor × adjustment_factor × (0.5 + 0.25 × history_depth + 0.25 × quote_coverage)`, where membership factors are 1/0.5/0.25/0 for good/degraded/poor/unusable (0.5 for unknown), adjustment is 1 for adjusted prices and 0.5 otherwise, and depth is prior market-volume sample count divided by the configured minimum, capped at 1. Stress evidence quality is `(number of active stress components / 7) × (0.5 + 0.5 × liquidity evidence quality)`, so valid upstream stress evidence can remain available when liquidity is unavailable. Low coverage, partial membership, missing quotes/history, invalid quotes, stale data, unknown/raw price adjustment, and missing stress context remain visible. `None` means unavailable; an explicit zero volume is preserved as zero. Nonfinite arithmetic results are suppressed. These quality measures describe the **evidence**, not confidence in future returns. A supplied upstream context must match the exact clock, including when other components are available.

`apply_liquidity_stress_to_snapshot` fills schema-1.0 `liquidity_score`, `stress_score`, stable `liquidity.*` and `stress.*` finite scalar names, active weights, contributors, flags, and data quality. It preserves other feature results and leaves `market_regime` unknown. Names ending `_proxy` identify proxies; `quoted_spread` is computed directly from a valid quote. The result and snapshot retain their raw components for inspection. See `mce/features/stress/snapshot.py` for the exhaustive name mapping.

The package does not reconstruct an order book, infer NBBO history, estimate hidden liquidity or actual transaction costs, fit a full intraday seasonality model, incorporate float for turnover, or predict outcomes. Liquidity horizons are the current intraday/session cumulative interval, the configurable prior same-clock trading-session lookback for headline RVOL, and separate 5- and 20-session same-clock RVOL diagnostics when enough history exists. The stress dependency graph is acyclic: causal observations → liquidity/gaps; earlier feature results → stress composite; prior stress observations → descriptive history.
