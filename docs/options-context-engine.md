# Options and implied-volatility context (MCE-17)

MCE does not price options. A separate options engine owns option valuation, implied-volatility solving, Greeks, and surface fitting. MCE consumes already computed, provider-neutral options evidence through `OptionsMarketContextInput`; `OptionsContextAdapter` parses JSON-compatible version-1.0 payloads without importing that engine. `OptionsContextFeatureEngine.compute(inputs, T, realized_volatility_context=..., historical_options_context=...)` is deterministic, has no provider or wall-clock calls, and returns typed descriptive context. The optional `apply_options_context_to_snapshot` projection adds `options.*` evidence to snapshot schema 1.0. It does not alter MCE-4 realized volatility, MCE-9 stress, deterministic regimes, or the statistical feature schema.

## Contract and clocks

The input requires `options_context_schema_version="1.0"` and `iv_units="annualized_decimal"`; unknown incompatible versions or units fail validation. IV `0.24` means 24% **annualized decimal volatility**. A value such as `24` is valid only if the provider intentionally means 2400% annualized; MCE does not guess units or silently clip finite raw values. Every ATM and surface point carries actual expiration, positive calendar-day DTE, annualized decimal IV, source method, observation and availability timestamps, and quality. ATM points declare `combined`, `call`, or `put` semantics. Surface wings declare `put`/`call`, signed coordinate, and exactly one coordinate system: signed delta, log moneyness, or relative moneyness. MCE never derives a delta from strike data. A supplied positive underlying price is optional; its absence is flagged. Duplicate tenor/combination or surface coordinates, negative/nonfinite IV, invalid DTE/expiration, bad wing signs, and inconsistent clocks are rejected. Unknown additive top-level source fields are ignored after version checking.

The source record preserves `source_engine`, semantic `source_engine_version`, optional `source_fingerprint`, `calculation_mode`, `market_data_mode`, source `as_of_ns`, market observation `data_as_of_ns`, effective `available_at_ns`, source quality, and surface diagnostics. `market_data_mode` is `live`, `delayed`, `eod`, or `synthetic`; synthetic means research input, never a claim of live liquidity. Source `as_of_ns` is a calculation clock, `data_as_of_ns` is the market observation clock, and `available_at_ns` is when the calculation became usable. At MCE clock `T`, all three source clocks and every point clock must be at or before `T`; the contract also requires point clocks no later than source clocks. Market-data age is `T−data_as_of_ns`, not fetch/cache age. EOD-derived context is not live intraday context. Delayed and EOD inputs keep raw evidence but cannot produce headline skew, VRP proxy, or options stress. Source and point staleness are checked against the configured maximum market age. A later source revision cannot rewrite an earlier snapshot because its availability clock was after that snapshot.

Only configured broad and optional sector underlyings are consumed. The latest causally available record for each symbol is selected by market observation time then availability time; contradictory same-clock revisions fail. Input ordering does not change results. `coverage = available configured underlyings / configured underlyings`. The primary broad reference is the first headline-eligible broad source in configuration order, falling back to the first available broad source for raw context. Sectors are optional. Sector relative IV, downside skew, and short-minus-long term spread subtract the primary broad value when both operands exist. ATM IV dispersion is the population standard deviation across headline-eligible broad and sector underlyings; fewer than two yields missing.

## Formula and availability reference

All IV/RV/skew/term quantities below are annualized **decimal volatility** or differences thereof. `None` means unavailable; zero means measured zero. Raw values may survive a quality gate while named headline values are withheld.

| Output | Formula and range | Required observations; missing behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `options.iv.atm` | Supplied combined ATM IV closest to configured target DTE; nonnegative annualized decimal | A combined ATM point. Actual `atm_tenor_days` stays in the typed result; MCE does not interpolate. |
| `options.iv.percentile`, `options.iv.robust_z` | Midrank `(count(prior < current)+0.5 count(prior = current))/N` in `[0,1]`; `(current−median)/(1.4826 MAD)` | At least `minimum_history` prior available ATM values. Zero MAD gives z=0 only at the median, otherwise missing. |
| `options.iv_rv.spread` | `ATM IV − trailing RV`; signed decimal volatility | Same-clock MCE-4 daily close-to-close RV window with coverage ≥0.8 and comparable maturity. Missing on no match. |
| `options.iv_rv.ratio` | `ATM IV / trailing RV`; nonnegative unitless | Same match; missing if RV ≤`1e−12` or quotient nonfinite. Zero RV can still yield a spread. |
| `options.vrp_proxy` | Equal to IV/RV spread; signed decimal volatility | Same match **and** headline options source quality. It is IV versus **trailing** realized volatility, not an ex-ante expected variance risk premium or a forecast. |
| `options.iv_rv.horizon_mismatch_days` | `abs(option calendar DTE − RV equivalent calendar days)`; nonnegative days | A same-clock eligible RV window. The nearest daily close-to-close window is chosen by this distance, using `RV sessions × 365/252`. `exact_match` means ≤0.5 day; `comparable` means ≤configured tolerance (default 7 days). Outside tolerance, spread/ratio/proxy are missing and a mismatch flag is set. Intraday RV is never substituted. |
| `options.term.short_long_spread` | `short IV − long IV`; signed decimal volatility, positive means inversion | At least `minimum_tenors` reliable combined ATM maturities (default 2). Short and long are smallest/largest actual DTE. Long IV zero makes ratio missing, but spread remains. |
| `options.term.short_long_ratio` | `short IV / long IV`; nonnegative unitless | Same maturities; missing if long IV ≤`1e−12` or quotient nonfinite. |
| `options.term.slope` | OLS `Σ(DTE−mean DTE)(IV−mean IV)/Σ(DTE−mean DTE)²`; IV per **calendar day** | At least two reliable distinct actual DTE values; no ordinal-tenor regression. Missing on degenerate or nonfinite result. |
| `options.term.curvature` | `middle IV − [short IV + (long IV−short IV)(middle DTE−short DTE)/(long DTE−short DTE)]`; signed decimal volatility | At least three reliable maturities. Middle is the interior tenor nearest target DTE. Positive means the middle is above the straight line through endpoints. |
| `options.skew.downside`, `options.skew.upside` | `put wing IV−ATM IV`, `call wing IV−ATM IV`; signed decimal volatility | Same expiration as ATM and configured symmetric wing coordinate (default ±0.25 signed delta), within coordinate tolerance. Each side is independently missing if absent, low quality, or wrong coordinate system. Snapshot skew headlines require overall source quality and both compatible wings; a lone wing remains raw typed evidence. |
| `options.skew.risk_reversal_call_minus_put` | **Call wing IV − put wing IV**; signed decimal volatility | Compatible same-expiration wing pair. Positive means call wing IV is higher. No opposite-sign alias is emitted. |
| `options.skew.butterfly` | `(put wing IV+call wing IV)/2−ATM IV`; signed decimal volatility | Compatible same-expiration pair. Positive means average wings exceed ATM. |
| `options.skew.atm_call_minus_put` | `ATM call IV−ATM put IV`; signed decimal volatility | Matched call/put ATM points for exactly the combined ATM expiration. It is raw microstructure-sensitive context, not an arbitrage signal. |
| `options.term.percentile`, `options.skew.percentile`, `options.iv_rv.percentile` | Same prior-only midrank convention as ATM IV; `[0,1]` | Respectively short-long spread, downside skew, and IV/RV spread history. At least `minimum_history` defined prior values of that measure. Robust z uses the same MAD rule. |
| `options.iv.dispersion` | Population std of eligible configured ATM IV values; nonnegative decimal volatility | At least two headline-eligible broad/sector underlyings. This is options IV dispersion, separate from MCE-8 return dispersion. |

The RV annualization comes from MCE-4. Its window counts XNYS trading sessions, while option DTE counts calendar days. `365/252` is an explicit approximation for matching horizons; it is not a forward-volatility estimate. IV-RV spread is not automatically a trading opportunity or evidence of mispricing. Trailing RV is not a future-vol forecast.

## Historical baseline and options stress

`OptionsContextHistory` is immutable and sorted by observation time. Duplicate `(symbol, as_of_ns)` observations are rejected so source revisions cannot create ambiguous baselines. A query uses only points with `observation < T` and `available_at_ns ≤ T`, then takes the configured most recent `history_window` (default 60). A current or future observation never enters its own percentile. Historical medians and robust z values are descriptive. The provider/orchestrator supplies history explicitly; no hidden mutable cache, database, or forecast is introduced.

`options.stress.iv = max(0, 2×ATM IV percentile−1)`, `options.stress.skew = max(0, 2×downside-skew percentile−1)`, and `options.stress.dislocation = max(0, 2×IV/RV-spread percentile−1)`. Each lies in `[0,1]` when its causal baseline exists and is missing otherwise. `options.stress.term = clamp(max(short_minus_long,0)/term_inversion_scale,0,1)` uses the **current options IV term structure**, default scale 0.10 decimal volatility. This score transform clips only normalized stress, never raw IV or raw spreads. A high IV/RV spread is a dislocation **relative to its own history**, not guaranteed mispricing. The dislocation component does not reuse MCE-9 volatility or stress.

The underlying score is the weighted mean of available components when at least `minimum_stress_components` (default two) exist. Default original weights are IV .30, skew .25, term .25, and dislocation .20; active weights are renormalized and retained in `OptionsStressContext.active_weights`. Otherwise the score is missing. The broad-market `options.stress.score` is the median of defined broad-underlying scores if their fraction meets `minimum_broad_coverage` (default .67). Sector scores remain per underlying. All scores are in `[0,1]`. Options stress is descriptive options-market evidence, **not crash probability** and not a replacement for MCE-9 stress.

## Quality, circular evidence, and snapshot

Surface metadata retains tenor/strike coverage, point count, stale/crossed contract counts, interpolation/extrapolation, own-contract fallback, and independent-neighbor count. Poor/unusable wing points are excluded from skew; fewer than the configured reliable surface count, coverage below 0.5, or zero independent neighbors marks a sparse surface. A stale source or component point, crossed quote indicator, extrapolated surface, EOD/delayed mode, poor source/ATM quality, sparse surface, or own-contract fallback withholds headline skew, VRP proxy, and options stress. Interpolation alone is preserved as metadata rather than silently treated as a new MCE estimate. Missing underlying price is flagged; raw IV can still be useful. Own-contract IV used as its own supposed independent fair-volatility evidence is circular and explicitly flagged. MCE does not reinterpret it as independent evidence.

The typed result retains the full source contract, raw ATM/term/skew/IV-RV measurements, actual tenor, source clocks, quality flags, per-underlying market-data age, history diagnostics, and active stress weights. Low-quality raw values remain available for audit; a missing headline is `None`, not zero. The snapshot uses stable `options.iv.*`, `options.iv_rv.*`, `options.vrp_proxy`, `options.term.*`, `options.skew.*`, `options.stress.*`, and `options.underlying.<SYMBOL>.*` names. It records source metadata and market observation freshness separately. Projection replaces a prior `options.*` namespace on replay and leaves all other fields, including categorical regimes, intact. Detailed source contracts remain in the typed feature set; the scalar snapshot is intentionally compact.

The implementation is transport-neutral and introduces no API keys, URLs, provider calls, pricing formulas, trade recommendations, broker integration, or execution. The synthetic fixtures in `tests/fixtures/options_scenarios.py` and manual benchmark in `benchmarks/options_context.py` need no external data. The benchmark reports adapter/validation and component/engine/projection timings on five underlyings, seven tenors, 700 surface points, 300 historical points, and configurable snapshots; it is a local measurement, not a production latency guarantee. MCE-18 macroeconomic/event/rates context remains deferred.
