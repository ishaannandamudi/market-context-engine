# Realized volatility engine (MCE-4)

`RealizedVolatilityEngine(config, calendar).compute(store, as_of_ns)` reads canonical bars from one configured source and returns an immutable `RealizedVolatilityFeatureSet`. It makes no provider calls and reads no wall clock. `apply_volatility_to_snapshot(result, snapshot=None)` adds the continuous evidence to schema `1.0` snapshots. It leaves categorical `volatility_regime` unknown and does not forecast volatility.

## Units and availability

Prices are positive finite decimals. Every public field called `annualized_volatility`, `rv_short`, `rv_medium`, `rv_long`, `market_realized_volatility`, or carrying an estimator name is **annualized decimal standard deviation**: `0.20` means 20% per year. `variance_per_session` is squared log-return variance for one trading session. `sum_squared_returns`, `realized_variance`, `bipower_variation`, `jump_variation`, and semivariances are **unannualized squared log-return sums** over their stated window. A gap magnitude is an absolute log return. Ratios and normalized scores are dimensionless. Unavailable values are `null`, not zero.

All observations require `effective_timestamp_ns <= as_of_ns`. A bar must also be complete: `bar_end_ns <= as_of_ns`. Only bars from `config.provider.source` count. Session bounds and previous trading dates come from XNYS, so weekends, holidays, DST changes, and early closes use the exchange schedule. Daily OHLC is read from an effective bar exactly spanning the full regular session, or from an exact, gap-free sequence of configured-cadence bars covering that session. The current day's high, low, and close do not enter daily estimators before its close. If a required session is absent, the daily window is unavailable; the reader does not skip across it or synthesize OHLC. A delayed final bar remains unavailable until its effective time.

## Estimators

Let `A` be `trading_sessions_per_year` (default 252), `n` the number of daily returns or daily OHLC bars as indicated, and `r_t = ln(P_t/P_(t-1))`. Invalid or nonfinite prices yield no estimate. All daily estimators return `sqrt(A × variance_per_session)`. They do not scale a multi-day return as if it were a one-day return.

| Estimator | Per-session variance and required input | Minimum | Assumptions and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Close to close | `s² = Σ(r_i − mean(r))²/(n−1)` over adjacent completed daily **close** returns; separately exposes raw `Σr_i²` | 2 returns, 3 consecutive completed sessions | Familiar sample-volatility estimate; includes overnight movement but loses within-session paths. `Σr_i²` is not called sample variance. |
| Parkinson | `Σ[ln(H_i/L_i)]²/(4n ln 2)` from daily high/low | 1 valid completed session | Efficient under an ideal continuous diffusion with little drift; ignores opening jumps and close location. |
| Garman–Klass | `mean{0.5[ln(H/L)]² − (2ln 2−1)[ln(C/O)]²}` from daily OHLC | 1 valid completed session | Uses open/close and range; idealized no significant opening jumps or drift. |
| Rogers–Satchell | `mean{ln(H/O)ln(H/C) + ln(L/O)ln(L/C)}` from daily OHLC | 1 valid completed session | Tolerates drift better than some range formulas; still misses overnight variance. |
| Yang–Zhang | `s²_overnight + k s²_open-close + (1−k) mean(RS)` where `k = 0.34/[1.34+(n+1)/(n−1)]`, overnight returns `ln(O_i/C_(i−1))`, open-close returns `ln(C_i/O_i)`, and both `s²` use denominator `n−1` | 3 adjacent trading-session pairs, 4 completed sessions | Combines overnight, open-close and drift-tolerant range terms. A missing previous close breaks the window. Previous means previous **XNYS trading** session, including across weekends and holidays. |

For a daily horizon `Nd`, close-to-close and Yang–Zhang consume `N+1` consecutive session OHLC summaries, producing `N` returns/pairs. The other range estimators consume the most recent `N` session summaries. Default horizons are `5d`, `10d`, `20d`, and `60d`. Range estimates on a single bar can be calculated by their pure functions, but configured engine horizons are at least two sessions.

### Intraday realized variation

For the configured cadence (default one minute), a completed regular-session bar supplies a close. The first session return uses the first available bar's open; an elapsed window starting later uses the latest completed close at most one cadence before its boundary as an anchor. Subsequent returns use the previous **observed** close. Without an anchor, the later elapsed-window estimate is unavailable. A missing interval can create one return spanning several intervals. It is not a synthesized zero return. `RV = Σr_i²`, `downside_semivariance = Σr_i² I(r_i<0)`, and `upside_semivariance = Σr_i² I(r_i>0)`; their sum equals RV for finite returns. `downside_share = downside_semivariance/RV` when `RV>0`.

Finite-sample bipower variation for `m >= 2` observed returns is `BV = (π/2) × [m/(m−1)] × Σ_(i=2..m)|r_i||r_(i−1)|`. `jump_variation = max(RV−BV, 0)` and `jump_share = jump_variation/RV` when defined. Bipower is only a rough continuous-path proxy under suitable sampling; this difference is not a definitive jump test.

For a window with boundary `B`, `E = max(0, floor((min(as_of, regular_close)−B)/cadence))` is the number of configured-cadence intervals that should have completed, and `D = E × cadence` is the elapsed trading time used for scaling. `S` is the actual regular-session duration, 390 minutes on an ordinary full day and shorter on an early close. The annualized intraday volatility is `sqrt(RV × A × S/D)` when `E>0`. This scales observed window variation to a session, then to a trading year. `observed_return_count/E` is coverage. Gaps, including missing trailing bars, lower coverage and raise `INSUFFICIENT_WINDOW_COVERAGE` below the configured threshold. The available observed estimate remains visible with that flag; consumers can apply stricter gates. There is no intraday estimate without a return. Default windows are `5m`, `15m`, `30m`, `1h`, and the elapsed current session. Five-minute bars work by configuring a five-minute cadence.

This scaling does not correct normal intraday U-shaped volatility seasonality. Minute-bar RV can be biased by market microstructure noise. Minute-of-day normalization, realized kernels, pre-averaging, and subsampling are deferred.

## Continuous context

The default short, medium, and long values are the `5d`, `20d`, and `60d` close-to-close annualized estimates. `short_long_ratio = short/long`, `medium_long_ratio = medium/long`, and `volatility_acceleration = (short−medium)/medium`. Denominators at or below `1e−12` yield `null`. `volatility_change_1d` is the difference between today's and the previous **trading-day endpoint's** rolling short annualized volatilities; it is not a change in raw intraday RV. Negative values mean lower recent volatility. All windows use completed sessions available by `as_of`.

For each completed endpoint, the engine derives up to 60 prior short-window close-to-close annualized values. **The current endpoint is excluded** from this reference distribution. At least 20 prior values are required by default. The percentile is `(count(prior < current) + 0.5 × count(prior = current))/count(prior)`, in `[0,1]`; ties use midrank. Robust `z = (current − median(prior))/(1.4826 × median(|prior−median(prior)|))`. A zero MAD yields zero when current equals the median and `null` otherwise; nonfinite results are unavailable. `volatility_shock_score = max(0, 2 × percentile − 1)`; this is an upward rank surprise, not a probability. The percentile itself is the extremeness/elevation score.

For a complete `Nd` window, the overnight and regular-session explanatory shares use squared returns `g_i² = [ln(O_i/C_(i−1))]²` and `d_i² = [ln(C_i/O_i)]²`: `overnight_share = Σg_i²/(Σg_i²+Σd_i²)` and `intraday_share = 1−overnight_share`. Both are zero when both sums are zero. `latest_gap_magnitude = |ln(O_last/C_previous)|`. The gap is not relabeled as volatility.

Estimator outputs remain separate. A descriptive consensus over available, comparable annualized estimates reports their median, `max−min` range, population standard deviation, and `agreement = 1/(1 + std/median)` (agreement is 1 if all are zero). This is not a substitute estimate and is not averaged into market volatility. Missing optional estimators stay `null`.

## Market aggregation, quality, and names

Each configured broad-market symbol has independent daily and intraday results, counts, coverage, flags, and contributors. Market realized-volatility **context** is the weighted mean of available symbols' medium close-to-close annualized volatilities: `Σw_i vol_i/Σw_i`; it is not an investable index's volatility. Weights default to equal and can be configured for every symbol. The aggregate requires the configured minimum fraction of symbols (default 0.67). `volatility_score` is the same available-symbol weighted mean of **prior-only short-window percentiles**, with the same symbol coverage gate. It measures observed elevation on `[0,1]`; it is neither raw nor predicted volatility. Contributors to this score carry precisely each symbol's weighted term. A missing symbol reduces coverage and adds `PARTIAL_UNIVERSE` and `LOW_COVERAGE`; remaining symbol records stay available. Cross-index volatility range and max/min ratio describe medium-volatility disagreement only; ratio is `null` for a near-zero minimum.

Daily and intraday windows report observed and expected counts and coverage. Insufficient windows, low coverage, stale latest bars, and missing symbols are flagged with existing quality enums. Staleness uses the configured bar freshness limit at the explicit `as_of`. The typed result holds all estimator components; snapshot `feature_values` holds only finite scalar projections. Names use `volatility.<metric>.<SYMBOL>.<horizon>`, such as `volatility.parkinson.SPY.20d`, `volatility.realized.SPY.5m`, and `volatility.jump_share.SPY.session`; symbol-wide names use `volatility.<metric>.<SYMBOL>`, such as `volatility.percentile.SPY`. The market-wide names are `volatility.cross_index_range` and `volatility.cross_index_ratio`. Snapshot direction/index scores from trend remain untouched.

`features.volatility` is frozen typed configuration. It includes `enabled`, named intraday horizons, daily horizon lengths, short/medium/long lengths, prior baseline length and minimum samples, bar cadence, annual trading sessions, minimum window and market coverage, optional range-estimator selection, and positive per-symbol index weights. The defaults are 5/20/60 short/medium/long sessions, a 60-session prior baseline with at least 20 samples, one-minute cadence, 252 annual sessions, 0.8 window coverage flag threshold, and 0.67 market coverage gate. All volatility settings are included in the result-relevant configuration fingerprint. `MCE_FEATURES__VOLATILITY__TRADING_SESSIONS_PER_YEAR=250` illustrates nested environment configuration. Market symbol weights must name every configured broad-market symbol when supplied.

The reader bounds history by the configured long and baseline windows. It currently recomputes completed-session summaries for each snapshot; a future deterministic, explicitly invalidatable cache can optimize repeated minute snapshots. Realized volatility of volatility is also deferred pending a clearly sampled causal series. Neither is needed for current measurements.

The Yang–Zhang construction follows [Yang and Zhang (2000)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=229190); the bipower interpretation follows [Barndorff-Nielsen and Shephard (2004)](https://shephard.scholars.harvard.edu/publications/power-and-bipower-variation-stochastic-volatility-and-jumps). The explicit finite-sample `m/(m−1)` factor and all availability choices above are MCE-4 conventions.
